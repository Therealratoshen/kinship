# Kinship — decision log

Dated, append-only. Mirrors the discipline used in the frozen PM decision log in
`Solana-project-for-world-fair`: an entry is never edited or deleted. When a decision
reverses, a new dated entry names the old one and says why. Anything contradicted here is
fair game to catch; anything silently rewritten is not.

**Reversibility key:** 🟢 reversible · 🟡 reversible with cost · 🔴 hard to undo.
