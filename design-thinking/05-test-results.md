## B · Real test plan (run this — predictions fixed as of 2026-10-17)

**Question under test (one, per protocol):** *Does a supervised event-ops fleet produce the feeling-shift overwhelmed→in-control in a real organiser — and the behavioral sign of trust (kept authority, escalated autonomy, second event)?*

**Prototype + task:** P1 storyboard becomes real as shadow mode on Filbert's next actual event (any lane of the PRD phase-2). Task for users: "Run your next event the way you do now — the fleet only drafts; approve or ignore each card."

**Users (3–5):** Filbert (baseline, n=1 honest bias note), + 2 chapter organisers (AIClub partner lane, after consent), + 1 organiser outside the network (bias correction), optional +1 Rizky-type member observing the agent in the group.

**Feeling probes:**
- Semantic pair: *overwhelmed ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█ in control* — rated day before, day after event #1, day after event #2.
- Second pair for the trust axis: *dread-of-ai-mistake ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█ confident*.
- Behavioral signs checklist: (1) re-runs fleet for event #2 without being asked; (2) requests autonomy *increase* for any lane; (3) tells another organiser unprompted; (4) abandons the spreadsheet/forward workaround; (5) grants WA write for reminders.

**Interview guide (15 min, semi-structured — stories not opinions):**
1. Warm-up: "Tell me about your last event — week before to week after."
2. "Walk me through the moment it felt heaviest. What exactly were you doing?"
3. "Show me what you used to manage any of that — bots, sheets, forwards."
4. "Have you ever let an AI post to your members? What happened?" (extreme probe: best AND worst)
5. "What would make you *not* use this again?" (negative-space question — the most important one)
6. Close: "Anything I should have asked?"

**Kill/continue thresholds (pre-committed):**
- CONTINUE-TO-BUILD if ≥2/3 organisers run event #2 with the fleet AND ≥1 requests an autonomy increase.
- REPOSITION if approval-gated works but member-facing identity (frame 3) triggers complaints → back-office wedge (PRD §2.4 disqualifying answer, handled as designed).
- KILL this approach if no second-event behavior after 4 weeks on real events — feeling never moved, polite praise or silence both count as kill.

## Iteration decision (as of Stage 5 close, evidence base: desk research + simulated dry-run)

**Continue — with the gate that the real test plan must run before any platform build.** Rationale: POV held up against every one of the six reports (no simulated finding contradicted it; E6/E1/E3 triangulate). No new need surfaced that contradicts the POV → not back-to-Define. Nothing killed at this stage because nothing was shipped; **S1-as-shadow-mode is the cheapest possible conversion of assumption to evidence, and it costs ~one event cycle.** Sensitivity: the decision rests on one unverified behavioral claim (caught-near-miss → trust). That is precisely what the test plan measures first.
