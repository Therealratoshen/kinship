**Read on 2026-10-22.** Every claim carries the URL it came from and the date it was read.
Anything I could not verify is in the last section, not in the body.

## A. The award

The official source is the Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy:
`dubaichambers.com/en/web/dubai-chamber-of-digital-economy/create-ai-agents` (read 2026-10-22).

It is the **Create AI Agents Championship**, announced 2026-10-07 by Dubai Crown Prince Sheikh Hamdan bin
Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, and it replaces the earlier Create Apps Championship
(`middleeastainews.com/p/dubai-launches-aed25m-create-ai-agents`, read 2026-10-22).

| Category | Prize | What the official site says it wants |
|---|---|---|
| **Best Societal AI Agent** | USD 150,000 | "For AI Agents designed to create meaningful impact across society. Can your agent improve communities, society or everyday life? Build a solution that addresses a real societal challenge and creates positive change in Dubai and beyond." |
| Best Personal AI Agent | USD 150,000 | "For AI Agents that enhance everyday life through personalised assistance, productivity and accessibility." |
| Best Business AI Agent | USD 150,000 | "For AI Agents that solve business challenges, streamline operations, boost productivity and unlock new opportunities for growth." |
| Best Youth AI Agent | USD 150,000 | "For AI Agents developed by young innovators who demonstrate creativity, technical talent and forward-thinking ideas." Under 21 at registration |
| **Dubai AI Agent of the Year** | USD 270,000+ | Additional grand prize for the overall winner, selected from the four category winners |

The grand-prize criterion, from the official site: the overall winner is chosen for "outstanding innovation,
impact and potential", and the finalists "Pitch to UAE government leaders, investors and industry
decision-makers, and showcase a solution built in Dubai, for the world."

**Note on the currency.** The Dubai Chamber site states **USD 150,000** per category (matching the award
graphic). The Dubai Media Office announcement states **AED 550,000** per category winner and **AED 1 million**
for AI Agent of the Year, with a total pool "exceeding AED 2.5 million". Both were read on 2026-10-22 and
the two forms are consistent — AED 550,000 is approximately USD 150,000, and AED 1 million is approximately
USD 270,000. The award graphic Filbert supplied states USD 150,000 and USD 270,000+.

## B. The Societal criteria, in the words of the official sources

Two official phrasings, both read 2026-10-22:

- **Dubai Chamber** (`dubaichambers.com/en/web/dubai-chamber-of-digital-economy/create-ai-agents`):
  *"For AI Agents designed to create meaningful impact across society. Can your agent improve communities,
  society or everyday life? Build a solution that addresses a real societal challenge and creates positive
  change in Dubai and beyond."*
- **Dubai Media Office** (`protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-create-ai-agents-championship-to-inspire-transformative-ideas-for-global-good/`):
  *"The Best Societal AI Agent category recognises solutions that create positive social impact by
  strengthening social cohesion and inclusion and supporting education, sustainability, and social
  resilience."*

The award graphic Filbert supplied states: *"Recognising AI Agents that address meaningful social challenges,
improve lives and create positive, lasting impact on communities."*

### What the criteria actually ask for, read closely

Three things recur across every official source and every secondary source:

1. **A real societal challenge, named.** Not "AI for community management" — a specific, describable
   difficulty, with the community affected.
2. **Demonstrated impact.** The Dubai Media Office framing: participants are expected to *"identify a
   priority problem, design an agent to address it, test its impact and explore how it could become a
   scalable product, service or business."* The word **test** is doing work: a pilot, a measurement, an
   evaluation.
3. **Scalability beyond the pilot.** *"explore how it could become a scalable product, service or business."*

And the grand-final framing adds a fourth: **"showcase a solution built in Dubai, for the world"** — the
final is in Dubai, in front of UAE government leaders and investors.

## C. Submission requirements

From `middleeastainews.com/p/dubai-launches-aed25m-create-ai-agents` and
`tbreak.com/create-ai-agents-championship-dubai/` (both read 2026-10-22):

- **A prototype, a pitch deck, and a demo video.** `tbreak.com` is explicit: "Registration closes in
  December 2026, with a prototype, pitch deck and demo video required."
- **Entry maturity:** individuals and teams may enter with *"an innovative idea, a prototype or early-stage
  MVP"*; existing businesses may submit *"a new solution or feature based on AI agents."*
- **Not eligible:** *"Fully commercialised solutions are not eligible."* (`middleeastainews.com`,
  `thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/10/07/...`, both read 2026-10-22.)

## E. Eligibility

- **Open worldwide.** *"Open to participants of all ages and backgrounds worldwide"*
  (`thenationalnews.com`, read 2026-10-22); *"open to everyone worldwide, regardless of technical
  background"* (`middleeastainews.com`). Both secondary sources agree.
- **No minimum age**, except the Youth category's own under-21 rule.
- **Technical and non-technical applicants both welcome** (`businesswire.com`, 2026-10-08).
- **A "solution built in Dubai"** is what the grand final showcases — this is about the showcase and the
  ecosystem, and the sources do not state an entity-incorporation requirement.

## F. Prior winners — the calibration signal

**None could be found.** The championship was announced on 2026-10-07 and the first grand final is in the
first week of May 2027, so no category has been awarded and no shortlist has been published as of
2026-10-22. The earlier competition it replaces (Create Apps Championship) awarded app categories, not
agent categories, so its winners are not a like-for-like comparison.

**This is a real gap in the evidence, not an oversight.** It means there is no public precedent for what
"good" looks like in this category, and the criteria must be read as the whole specification.

## G. What this means for a Kinship submission

The Societal criteria and Kinship's own thesis line up more closely than the award's language suggests.
"Strengthening social cohesion and inclusion" is a community problem; "positive, lasting impact on
communities" is a community problem; and "test its impact" is a problem for a system whose entire claim is
that its actions are *logged and replayable* — because a system that can replay its own log is the one kind
of system whose impact claim can be checked rather than asserted.

The gap in Kinship's position is not the thesis. It is the **evidence**: Kinship has no shipped transport
(D21), no dated event (D9), and no measured outcome. A prize whose criterion says "test its impact" and
whose final showcases a solution "built in Dubai, for the world" needs a community, a pilot, and a number
that is measured rather than claimed.

The most valuable thing Kinship could add to a submission is the thing the award asks for and the repo
currently cannot show: **one community, running the fleet in shadow mode, with the audit trail as the
artefact** — because the replayable log is the only artefact that turns "it helped" into a checkable claim.

## H. Could not verify

- **The exact registration deadline.** Every source says "December 2026" or "closes in December 2026"; no
  source I read gives a day. The official site, as read, does not state one either. Treat the last weeks of
  December 2026 as the working assumption and confirm directly with the Dubai Chamber.
- **Whether prior-year Create Apps Championship winners may compete**, and whether any judging rubric
  document (scoring weights, panel composition) exists. I found no published rubric — only the category
  descriptions quoted above.
- **Prize tax treatment, and any obligation to operate or register in the UAE.** No source I read addresses
  either.
- **Whether the demo video has a duration limit or format requirement.** `tbreak.com` states a demo video
  is required without giving a specification.
- **Alternative awards.** I did not complete a systematic sweep of other open-source, civic-tech or
  humanitarian-AI awards with deadlines in the next six months. The three research threads I ran covered the
  agent-landscape, the operating-model literature, and this award. If alternative targets matter, that sweep
  is still outstanding and should be treated as an open task rather than as a completed one.
